Frequently Asked Questions

What is doingnothing.lol?

It is an honest humor website where an unemployed founder does nothing for a living. Visitors voluntarily buy him a meal to feature their product, startup, or profile on today's public daily leaderboard.

Is this a charity or non-profit?

No, absolutely not. Contributions are personal gifts to buy actual food. It is not tax-deductible, and we are not a 501(c)(3) or charitable entity.

Are payments voluntary?

Yes, 100% voluntary. You can explore the leaderboard, roast your startup, or play the mini-games completely free.

What do I get when I contribute?

Your name, logo, link, and message are displayed on today’s live leaderboard. Contributions over $100 earn the permanent "Legend" title in the Hall of Fame.

How does the leaderboard reset?

The board resets every 24 hours at midnight UTC. This ensures new startups and creators have a chance to claim the top spot each day.

Can I contribute anonymously?

Yes. Check the "Contribute anonymously" box on checkout to be listed as "Anonymous Hero" with no external link.

Can I get a refund?

No refunds. Once the meal is funded, the meal is eaten and server costs are incurred.

What links and content are permitted?

We welcome genuine tools, creator portfolios, GitHub repos, and social handles. Any spam, scams, or illegal content is immediately purged.
