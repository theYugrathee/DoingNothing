A few honest questions

What should I do when I’m doing nothing?

If you need a real reset, get a glass of water, stretch, step outside for five minutes, or choose one tiny task. If you’re just here for a laugh, try the stillness challenge or excuse machine.

What is doingnothing.lol?

It’s a playful corner of the internet with tiny procrastination games, a stillness challenge, silly excuses, and a voluntary supporter board. It’s here to make a slow day a little more fun.

Do I have to pay to use the site?

No. The games and pages are free to use. Payments are optional, and the supporter board is for fun and recognition; a listing does not promise clicks, sales, or customers.

Board rules.

Here’s how the Doing Nothing supporter board works, in plain English.

Entries and rankings

Recent shows individual donations, newest first. Top Players groups donations by supporter and ranks each person by their lifetime total. Older entries remain available on later pages.

Payments and placement

Supporting the site is optional ($1 minimum, $3,000 maximum per transaction; larger amounts can be submitted in multiple contributions and combine automatically). All payments are final and strictly non-refundable once processed and listed. A listing is for fun and recognition and does not promise clicks, sales, customers, or other results.

Names, messages, and links

Use a name and link you’re allowed to share. Keep messages and links lawful and safe for work. The site may hide or remove spam, scams, unsafe links, or content that doesn’t fit these rules.

Questions? Email the founder.

Your privacy, plainly.

Public games do not require an account. The site uses browser storage for preferences and preview entries, so those preview entries stay in that browser.

Supporter entries

If you submit a live supporter entry, the name you choose, optional link and message, amount, and entry time may be displayed on the public board. The anonymous option hides your name, link, and logo from the public listing. The configured checkout opens an external payment page; payment details are handled by that provider.

Browser storage and analytics

Theme, sound, motion, and preview settings may be saved in your browser. This site also loads Umami analytics to understand basic site visits. For analytics details, see Umami’s privacy information.

Questions or removal requests

Contact founder@doingnothing.lol if you have a privacy question or want to ask about an entry.

Terms, without the legal fog.

Just-for-fun site

Doing Nothing is an entertainment site with browser games and a supporter board. Use the games for fun; they are not professional, financial, or health advice.

Optional payments

Payments are voluntary (minimum $1, maximum $3,000 per contribution). Contributions exceeding $3,000 can be submitted across multiple contributions and will automatically combine under your name/link on the leaderboard. Checkout is handled by the configured external payment provider and its terms also apply to that transaction.

Strict No-Refund Policy

All payments are strictly non-refundable once payment is completed and your link or entry is added to the leaderboard. Board placement and recognition are fulfilled immediately upon confirmation. Rule-breaking entries that are removed are not eligible for a refund.

Board listings

Recent lists donations individually; Top Players groups a supporter’s entries by lifetime total. Listings are moderated and may be hidden or removed if they break the board rules. A listing is recognition only—there is no promise of traffic, sales, or customers.

Questions about a payment or listing? Contact the founder.
