Frequently Asked Questions

What is doingnothing.lol?

It is an honest humor website where an unemployed founder does nothing for a living. Visitors voluntarily buy him a meal to feature their product, startup, or profile on today's public daily leaderboard.

Is this a charity or non-profit?

No, absolutely not. Contributions are personal gifts to buy actual food. It is not tax-deductible, and we are not a 501(c)(3) or charitable entity.

Are payments voluntary?

Yes, 100% voluntary. You can explore the leaderboard, roast your startup, or play the mini-games completely free. Paying is for supporters who want to help out or promote their project.

What do I get when I contribute?

Your name, logo/avatar, link, and message are displayed on today’s live leaderboard. Contributions over $100 earn the permanent "Legend" title and a permanent spot in the Hall of Fame.

How does the leaderboard reset?

The board resets every 24 hours at midnight UTC. This ensures new startups and creators have a chance to claim the top spot each day.

Can I contribute anonymously?

Yes. Check the "Contribute anonymously" box on checkout. You will be listed as "Anonymous Hero" with no link or logo attached.

Can I get a refund?

No refunds. Once the meal is funded or eaten, the funds are spent and server costs are incurred.

What links and content are permitted?

We welcome genuine SaaS tools, creator portfolios, GitHub repos, and social handles. Any spam, scams, phishing, or illegal content is immediately deleted without refund.

The 5 Rules of Doing Nothing

  1. Personal Gifts Only: Payments are voluntary and go to the founder personally. It is not a charity.
  2. Amount Ranking: The daily board ranks by amount contributed, highest first.
  3. Daily Reset: The board resets every day at midnight UTC. Daily top contributors enter the Hall of Fame.
  4. Fair Recognition: Placement is social recognition. We make no guarantees about traffic or revenue.
  5. Zero Tolerance for Spam: Any malicious links, scams, or hate speech are deleted immediately.
