A few honest questions

What should I do when I’m doing nothing?

If you need a real reset, get a glass of water, stretch, step outside for five minutes, or choose one tiny task. If you’re just here for a laugh, try the stillness challenge or excuse machine.

What is doingnothing.lol?

It’s a playful corner of the internet with tiny procrastination games, a stillness challenge, silly excuses, and a voluntary supporter board. It’s here to make a slow day a little more fun.

Do I have to pay to use the site?

No. The games and pages are free. A paid board listing is a separate purchase for a place on the supporter board; it does not promise clicks, sales, or customers.

Board rules.

Here’s how the Doing Nothing supporter board works, in plain English.

How entries and rankings work

Recent lists individual entries newest first, 100 per page. Top Players groups entries that share the same normalized website link, or the same name when there is no link, and ranks them by their recorded lifetime total. Anonymous entries are kept separate. Older entries can be browsed with the page controls.

What a payment buys

The games and pages are free. A payment buys only a place on the supporter board, with the name, link and message you submit (or an anonymous listing). It does not buy a meal, charity contribution, guaranteed position, impressions, clicks, customers or sales. This is not a charitable donation and no charitable receipt is issued. The current form has a $1 USD minimum and a $3,000 USD maximum per single transaction. To contribute more than $3,000, you can make multiple contributions using the same name and link—all amounts automatically combine into your lifetime total on the leaderboard. All payments are strictly final and non-refundable once processed and added to the leaderboard. Prices, currency and taxes at checkout control the transaction.

Age and permission

You must be 18 or older to make the purchase yourself. A person under 18 may submit a listing only with a parent or legal guardian’s permission, and that parent or guardian must complete checkout. Do not use another person’s payment account or payment method without their express permission.

What you may post

Only submit a name, link, logo or message that you have the right to share. Do not post unlawful, misleading, abusive, hateful, sexually explicit, spam, scam, malware, or phishing content, or someone else’s private or sensitive information. External links are provided by users and are not endorsements by this site.

Information you submit

When you fill in the supporter form, your browser temporarily handles the name, optional link, message, intended amount, anonymous choice and consent selection. In preview mode, new entries stay in this browser. With live checkout, the current form opens a separate payment link: the name, link, message and intended amount are not automatically sent to that provider or saved as a board entry. After a payment is independently confirmed, the founder may manually record a listing in Supabase. If recorded, the chosen name or anonymous label, link (if not anonymous), message, amount and entry time may be displayed publicly. Anonymous hides only your name and link. Contact the founder to request correction or removal.

Preview entries

When the site is in preview mode, new entries are stored in this browser and are not submitted to the supporter database. Browser storage can be cleared in your browser settings. Preview entries submitted before this notice was updated may have been sent to the database; contact the founder if you want one checked or removed.

Browser storage

This site uses local browser storage for preferences such as theme, sound and motion, preview entries, and your best stillness score. This information remains on your device unless you clear the site’s stored data.

Analytics and third-party services

These services are used to run the site, show the board, prevent abuse, and understand aggregate visits. Umami Cloud analytics receives page views, referrer information, browser, operating system and device type, and approximate location. Umami says its tracker does not use cookies; its cloud privacy notice explains its processing and operational logs. The app also uses Supabase to read and store supporter entries and site settings, Google Fonts for type, and a Cloudflare-hosted library for 3D graphics. When you enter a website link, the logo preview may request that site’s icon from Google’s favicon service and, if needed, DuckDuckGo. Those providers receive ordinary connection data when your browser requests their services. The site does not intentionally sell board form information to advertisers.

Read the providers’ notices: Umami, Supabase, Google, Cloudflare, and DuckDuckGo.
