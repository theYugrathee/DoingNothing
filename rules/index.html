Rules & Pricing.

Transparent rules, contribution tiers, and pin perks for the Doing Nothing daily leaderboard.

The 5 Rules of Doing Nothing

  1. Personal Gifts Only: Payments are voluntary and go to the founder personally. It is not a charity.
  2. Amount Ranking: The daily board ranks by amount contributed, highest first.
  3. Daily Reset: The board resets every day at midnight UTC. Daily top contributors enter the Hall of Fame.
  4. Fair Recognition: Placement is social recognition. We make no guarantees about traffic or revenue.
  5. Zero Tolerance for Spam: Any malicious links, scams, or hate speech are deleted immediately.
